# Public Vulnerability Case Studies & Disclosure Analysis

Learning from real-world bug bounty write-ups bridges the gap between simulated academy labs and complex production architectures.

---

## 🎯 Case Study Objectives
1. **Reverse Engineer Hacker Methodology**: How did the researcher discover the endpoint, formulate their hypothesis, and bypass defenses?
2. **Understand Root Architectural Flaws**: What assumptions made by engineering or product teams led to this vulnerability?
3. **Prioritize Recent Disclosures**: Focus heavily on public disclosures from **2025 and 2026** on HackerOne Hacktivity and Bugcrowd to understand contemporary attack surfaces (modern APIs, cloud setups, modern frameworks).

---

## ⚖️ Standards of Research
- **No Fabrications**: Never invent report IDs, bounty amounts, researcher names, or disclosure dates.
- **Always Cite Sources**: Every study must link to its verified, publicly accessible report URL.
- **Redaction**: Maintain redaction of sensitive client data or credentials referenced in public write-ups.
